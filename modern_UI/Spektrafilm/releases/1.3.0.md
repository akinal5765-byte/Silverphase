# Silverphase macOS 1.3.0（构建 5）

**中文 · [English](#english)**

2026-11-18 · Apple Silicon，macOS 15 或更高。[下载 DMG 与校验文件](https://github.com/akinal5765-byte/Silverphase/releases/tag/macos-v1.3.0)。

## macOS 编辑与导出

- **入片调整。** 在胶片曝光前调节局部亮部和暗部、入片曝光、对比度、低端提升、高端增益、中间调细节、色彩增强和饱和度。旧照片仍可读取原来的高光、阴影、白色色阶和黑色色阶。
- **匹配相机预览亮度。** 受支持的 RAW 可用相机预览拟合起始曝光，高光保护会限制提亮。滑杆标出起始值，后续微调由每张照片保存。复制相机设置会传递匹配开关，目标照片各自计算基准。关闭再开启可重新匹配。
- **模拟尺寸。** 位于「胶片 → 画幅 ⋯」菜单。重裁切后需要输出大图时，可指定裁切后长边，让颗粒和光晕在该尺寸上生成。使用原始尺寸适合日常编辑。应用在入片前重采样，导出配方的尺寸控制仍用于成片缩小。
- **编辑手感与交付。** 调整刻度尺的触控反馈，导出的 JPEG、TIFF 和 PNG 写入照片星级（`xmp:Rating`）。「关于」提供对应版本的更新说明，并列出实验胶片的数据来源与许可。

## 材料与共享引擎

- 「实验」增加 29 款 Fotufilm 胶片配置，由 Noah Haasis 的 Negs 转换，采用独立材料 ID。保留原有近似标记、来源与 CC BY-SA 4.0 授权。公开构建包含这些配置，私有材料通过独立的本地目录管理。
- 共享引擎统一了 DIR 曲线准备与运行阶段的饱和响应。Hanatos 光谱窗口以模型实际的中性光谱归一化，Cineon DI 的中性探针使用相同权重。颗粒柔化按当前渲染像素对应的胶片尺度换算。
- 更新 238 组彩色负片与印相材料的中性滤色配对。校准在 TH-KG3 下通过灰阶和独立半档样点筛选。已保存的照片重新渲染时，使用这些配对的成片可能出现变化。
- 原始实测数组与采用的数据范围见 [材料来源及许可](../../../license/DATA-SOURCES.md) 和 [可追溯记录](../../../engine/resources_product/UPSTREAM.json)。

## 验证范围

- Apple Silicon Release 构建通过。应用包的版本与构建号为 1.3.0（5），包含 63 个公开配置及随附许可。以 `SILVERPHASE_LOCAL_MATERIALS=0` 构建，签名与资源检查通过。
- macOS 全量 XCTest 共 588 项，540 项通过、0 项失败、48 项跳过。跳过项来自缺失的历史照片与未启用的实拍或性能专项。
- 用最终应用包的引擎资源运行 Metal 冒烟测试和 5219 回归，均通过。工程生成、开源资料审计与新增文档链接检查通过。

新增 [Kodak 5219 参考检查](../VALIDATION.md#5219-底片与光谱色样)，包含 14 个原厂曝光点、三个高密度蓝通道颗粒测量点和 24 个实测反射光谱色样。验证记录入片曝光、底片密度、相纸密度和输出 RGB。使用一次曝光对齐后，红、绿、蓝通道平均绝对密度误差为 0.034、0.012、0.008 D。相对直接光谱积分，色样的 RGB 重建误差平均为 0.102 EV，最大为 0.533 EV。三个高密度蓝通道颗粒测量值约低于原厂代表性曲线三分之一。

这些结果验证曲线形状、光谱近似和软件回归。实物胶片的绝对感光速度和端到端成片颜色仍需独立实验。

## English

November 18, 2026 · Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later. [DMG and checksums](https://github.com/akinal5765-byte/Silverphase/releases/tag/macos-v1.3.0).

### Editing and export
- Pre-film adjustments include local lights and darks, exposure, contrast, toe lift, shoulder gain, midtone detail, colour boost, and saturation. Old edits still read the previous highlights, shadows, whites, and blacks.
- For supported RAWs, camera-preview matching estimates a starting exposure with highlight protection. Each photo stores its own baseline and fine adjustment. Copying Camera settings transfers the toggle, not the baseline; off and on recalculates it.
- **Film → Format ⋯ → Simulation Size** sets the post-crop long edge used for grain and halation. Resampling occurs before film; export sizing can still reduce the finished image.
- Ruler feedback was refined. JPEG, TIFF and PNG carry the photo rating in `xmp:Rating`. About shows release notes and experimental-material origins and terms.

### Materials and engine
- The public build includes 29 Fotufilm stocks adapted by Noah Haasis's Negs, with independent IDs and retained approximation labels and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms. Private material overlays are separate.
- The engine aligns DIR preparation and runtime saturation response, normalizes the Hanatos window with the model's neutral spectrum, and shares those weights with the Cineon DI neutral probe. Grain softness scales with film units per render pixel.
- Neutral print filtration was recalibrated for 238 colour-negative and paper combinations under TH-KG3 using grey and independent half-stop points. Re-rendered edits may change when using an affected pair. [Data sources](../../../license/DATA-SOURCES.md) · [Provenance](../../../engine/resources_product/UPSTREAM.json).

### Verification
The Apple Silicon Release app passed signing and resource checks with `SILVERPHASE_LOCAL_MATERIALS=0` and 63 public profiles. Full XCTest: 540 passed, none failed, 48 skipped (588 total), due to missing fixtures or optional real-photo/performance checks. Metal smoke and Kodak 5219 regressions passed with final bundle resources, as did the project, open-source and document checks.

The new [Kodak 5219 reference checks](../VALIDATION.md#5219-底片与光谱色样) include 14 manufacturer exposure points, three high-density blue-channel granularity points, and 24 measured reflectance samples. After one exposure alignment, RGB channel mean absolute density errors were 0.034, 0.012, and 0.008 D. RGB reconstruction versus direct spectral integration averaged 0.102 EV, with a 0.533 EV maximum. The three blue-granularity points were roughly one third below the representative manufacturer curve. This tests curve shape, spectral approximation and regression, not absolute film speed or end-to-end physical print accuracy.

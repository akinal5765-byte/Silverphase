# Silverphase macOS 1.3.1（构建 6）

**中文 · [English](#english)**

源码版本适用于 Apple Silicon 和 macOS 15 或更高版本。安装包与签名说明见[下载指南](../../../docs/BINARY-RELEASES.md)。

- 「预处理」保留入片影调和 RAW 相机亮度匹配。「胶片」集中管理测光和手动胶片曝光，设置剪贴板也按这个分组传递。
- 29 款公开 Fotufilm 胶片均有封面与来源标识。移植数据、许可和封面来源见[材料说明](../../../license/DATA-SOURCES.md#experimental-fotufilm-profiles)。
- 共享显影引擎降低 17 款 Fotufilm 配置的 DIR 空间邻接强度。明暗交界处的亮侧过冲和暗侧欠冲得到抑制，均匀曝光下的 DIR 响应得到保留。重新渲染已有编辑时，这些胶片的局部边缘仍可能变化。

29 款胶片的原生 Metal 检查通过了 174 组合成边缘条件。Pro 160NS 在 120 间距、中间曝光阶跃下的越界幅度从 57.5% 降到 17.4%。40% 是这组测试的工程筛查线，实拍底片的边缘密度仍待验证。[完整测试与限制](../VALIDATION.md#fotufilm-dir-边缘增强复查)

公开资源配置的 Apple Silicon Debug 构建通过，已读取应用包中的版本号与构建号。

## English

Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. See the [download guide](../../../docs/BINARY-RELEASES.md) for installation and signing.

- Prep retains pre-film tone and RAW camera brightness matching. Film now groups metering and manual film exposure; the settings clipboard uses the same groups.
- All 29 public Fotufilm stocks have cover art and source badges. See [material credits and licences](../../../license/DATA-SOURCES.md#experimental-fotufilm-profiles).
- Development reduces the spatial DIR adjacency strength of 17 Fotufilm profiles, mitigating overshoot and undershoot around edges while retaining the response on uniformly exposed areas. Re-rendering old edits may change local edges.

Native Metal checks passed for 174 synthetic edge conditions across 29 stocks. Pro 160NS overshoot at the 120-spacing mid-exposure step dropped from 57.5% to 17.4%. The 40% threshold is an engineering screening value, not physical-film evidence. [Test details](../VALIDATION.md#fotufilm-dir-边缘增强复查). The Apple Silicon Debug build using public resources passed and its version and build were checked.

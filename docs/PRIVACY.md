# Silverphase 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-09

本政策适用于 Yukun Li 发布的 Silverphase iPhone 与 iPad 应用。

## 照片与编辑记录

Silverphase 在设备上处理照片。应用不会将你的原始照片、成片、拍摄元数据或编辑记录上传至开发者服务器，也未接入广告、跨应用追踪或第三方分析 SDK。应用不要求注册账户。

你通过系统照片选择器或文件选择器主动选择导入内容。导入的原文件会复制到应用内图库；照片名称、拍摄信息、编辑参数、评分和旗标可随图库保存在设备上，以便下次继续编辑。预览缩略图和渲染缓存用于改善本机使用体验。

使用应用内相机时，Silverphase 请求相机权限，拍摄原片保存在本地图库。使用锁屏相机时，原片暂存在系统提供的拍摄会话目录，解锁后由主应用导入本地图库。拍摄后写入系统照片需要添加照片权限。根据保存设置，应用可保存显影成片，或同时保存可用的 RAW 原片。

应用内相机的“记录拍摄位置”设置默认开启，但只有你授予系统使用期间定位权限后，才会在拍照时将最近有效的位置写入照片元数据。可在应用设置中关闭，或在系统设置中管理权限；没有后台定位。锁屏相机不请求定位。照片的位置信息可随导出保留或按导出设置移除，不会由应用自动发送给开发者。

## 权限与导出

从系统照片选择器导入时，应用接收你选择的项目；普通导入无需整库读取权限。为优先取得 RAW 原件，应用可能请求照片读取权限，并只查询所选照片的原始资源。如果原件在 iCloud，系统可按需下载到本机。你主动选择保存成片到系统照片图库时，应用会请求添加照片的权限。你可以在系统设置中管理权限，或选择导出到文件。

导出设置可以保留拍摄元数据及位置信息，也可以移除位置信息。请在分享前检查导出设置；接收成片的人或服务可能读取成片中保留的信息。导出到 iCloud Drive、其他云盘、系统照片图库或分享扩展时，相应系统功能及服务提供方的隐私规则适用。

## 偏好设置与本地诊断

应用在本地保存偏好设置、导出配方及预设。为帮助排查导出中断，应用还可保留最近一次导出的状态、耗时、内存情况、导出选项和错误信息。这些诊断不包含图像像素，不会由应用自动发送给开发者。系统是否向 Apple 或开发者提供系统级诊断，取决于你的系统设置和 Apple 的政策。

## 保留与删除

你可以在应用图库中移除导入的照片及对应编辑记录；这不会删除系统照片图库或导入前的原文件。缓存可能由应用或系统清理。删除应用通常会移除其本机数据；系统备份、iCloud 或其他服务中已有的副本按各自设置管理。已导出的文件及照片需要在相应保存位置另行删除。

Silverphase 不提供自建云端图库或跨设备同步；设备备份及系统云服务仍可能根据你的设置保存应用数据。

## 外部链接与支持

设置和关于页中的 Layerlight 推荐由你主动点击后打开 App Store，链接不带追踪参数。Silverphase 不向 Layerlight 传递你的照片或编辑记录；Layerlight 是独立应用，其数据处理适用自己的政策。

你可以通过 [Silverphase GitHub Issues](https://github.com/akinal5765-byte/Silverphase/issues) 联系维护者，提出隐私问题或请求支持。GitHub Issues 是公开渠道，请勿上传私人照片、定位信息、密码、证件或完整诊断日志。你自愿提交的信息将由 GitHub 依其政策处理，并由维护者用于回应你的请求。

应用的数据处理方式如有变化，本政策及相应商店声明会随之更新。

## English

This policy covers the Silverphase iPhone and iPad app distributed by Yukun Li.

**Photos and edits.** Silverphase processes photos on your device. The app does not upload originals, rendered photos, photo metadata or edit records to developer servers. It includes no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDK. No account is required. You choose imports through the system photo or file picker. Imported originals are copied into the app library. Names, photo information, edits, ratings and flags can remain locally so you can resume editing. Local thumbnails and rendering caches support the editing experience.

**Camera.** Silverphase requests camera access for in-app capture. Captured originals are stored in the local library. Lock-screen captures are staged in the system-provided capture session directory and imported by the app after unlock. Saving captures to Photos requires add-only access. Depending on your settings, the app can save the developed photo or also include the RAW original when available.

**Capture location.** The in-app “record capture location” setting is enabled by default. Tagging requires your system permission for location while using the app and uses a recent valid location at capture time. Disable it in app settings or manage permission in system settings. There is no background location access; locked capture does not request location. Export policy controls retaining or stripping photo location. The app does not automatically send locations to the developer.

**Permissions and exports.** Photo-picker imports can receive selected items without full library access. To prefer RAW originals, the app may request Photos read access and look up original resources for the selected items only. Photos can download an original from iCloud when needed. Saving an export to Photos requests add-only access. You can manage permissions in system settings or export to Files instead. Export settings control preservation of photo metadata and location. Review these settings before sharing: recipients may read retained metadata. Exports to Photos, iCloud Drive, other cloud providers or share extensions are also governed by the relevant system features and providers.

**Preferences and diagnostics.** Preferences, export recipes and presets are stored locally. A local record of the most recent export may contain status, elapsed time, memory use, export options and errors to help investigate interruptions. It contains no image pixels and is not automatically sent to the developer. System-level diagnostic sharing depends on your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

**Retention and deletion.** Removing an item from the app library removes the app-owned photo and edit record without deleting its source in Photos or Files. Caches may be cleared by the app or system. Deleting the app normally removes its local data; existing backups and cloud copies follow the corresponding settings. Previously exported items must be deleted from their destinations separately. Silverphase provides no developer-operated cloud library or cross-device sync, but system backups and cloud services may retain app data according to your settings.

**Links and support.** The optional Layerlight recommendation opens its App Store listing only when you select it, without tracking parameters. Silverphase does not pass photos or edit records to Layerlight. Layerlight is a separate app with its own policy. Contact the maintainer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/akinal5765-byte/Silverphase/issues). Issues are public: do not post private photos, location data, passwords, identity documents or complete diagnostic logs. GitHub processes submissions under its own policy; the maintainer uses information you voluntarily provide to respond to your request.

This policy and the store disclosures will be updated when the app's data handling changes.

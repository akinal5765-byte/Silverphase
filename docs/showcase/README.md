# Silverphase · Virtual darkroom showcase

[简体中文主页](../../README.md) · [English home](../../README.en.md) · [Downloads](../BINARY-RELEASES.md)

Four **2560 × 1600** sRGB cards. These are renders from the Silverphase Metal engine, not scans of physical prints and not current app-interface captures. Grain and halation are enabled on the simulated prints. The photography is from approved Silverphase project fixtures; the source RAW files are not distributed.

| Card | Recipe and comparison |
|---|---|
| [01 · Same negative, two papers](01-paper-study.png) | Lotus, Portra 400 at +0.67 EV from the metered baseline. A single cached negative is printed on Portra Endura and Supra Endura with the same print exposure value (0.88). Each paper uses its built-in neutral calibration. The RAW reference is Apple's display decode with no film simulation. The second row samples matching pixels directly from the 4096 px-wide renders. |
| [02 · Three scenes, three stocks](02-film-studies.png) | Lotus: Portra 400 / Portra Endura. White bird: Acros II / Ilford Multigrade IV RC. Storefront: CineStill 800T / Kodak Vision 2383. These are independent examples, not a controlled stock comparison. |
| [03 · After dark](03-800t-night.png) | CineStill 800T / Kodak Vision 2383, +0.67 EV from the metered film baseline, print exposure value 0.84, 35 mm grain at 1× and stock halation enabled. The underlying render is 7008 × 4672; the two details are copied at 1:1 with no enlargement or post-sharpening. |
| [04 · Beyond the frame](04-film-edge-covers.png) | Portra 400 lotus rendered with Silverphase's 135 strip mode. Perforations, edge markings and the frame number are generated by the engine, not overlaid in the layout. Four reduced cover images come from the in-app library. |

The three source photographs (lotus, white bird and night storefront) are the maintainer-approved project cases documented in the [2026-10-31 fixture and capture record](../../screenshots/current/manifest.json).

# Silverphase iOS 1.3.0（构建 10）

**中文 · [English](#english)**

适用于 iOS／iPadOS 18 或更高版本。版本号适用于主 App、控制中心相机入口与锁屏相机扩展。正式下载入口为 [中国大陆 App Store](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6829987567)或[其他地区 App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6829987567)，测试版通过 [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/uwY3ZYJp) 提供。各渠道的可安装版本以其页面为准。

## 编辑与导出

- 「相机」新增入片亮部、暗部、曝光、对比度、低端提升、高端增益、中间调细节、色彩增强和饱和度。旧照片可继续调整已有的高光、阴影、白色色阶和黑色色阶。
- 受支持的 RAW 可按相机内嵌预览匹配起始亮度。匹配保护高光，每张照片保存自己的基准和微调。关闭后再开启可重新匹配，复制设置会把匹配开关传给目标照片。
- 成片像素尺寸在「导出」中调整。已有自定义模拟尺寸的照片沿用原设置，可以从「胶片 → 工艺操作」恢复原始模拟尺寸。
- 调整刻度尺拖动时的触觉反馈频率。JPEG、PNG、TIFF 写入图库星级（`xmp:Rating`）。「关于」可查看版本更新与随包许可。

## 材料与引擎

「实验」包含 29 款由 Noah Haasis 的 Negs 转换的 Fotufilm 配置，采用 CC BY-SA 4.0 授权。iOS 共用 macOS 1.3.0 的 DIR、Hanatos 和 Cineon 中性探针、颗粒柔化与中性印相滤色更新。旧编辑使用更新后的印相配对再次渲染时，成片可能变化。详见[材料来源](../../license/DATA-SOURCES.md)与[桌面 1.3.0 发布说明](../../modern_UI/Spektrafilm/releases/1.3.0.md)。

## English

Requires iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. The app, Control Center camera entry, and locked camera extension share version 1.3.0 (build 10). Install via the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6829987567) or [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/uwY3ZYJp); availability depends on the channel.

### Editing and export
- New pre-film lights, darks, exposure, contrast, toe lift, shoulder gain, midtone detail, colour boost, and saturation. Existing photos retain their saved highlights, shadows, whites, and blacks.
- Supported RAWs can match the brightness of the camera's embedded preview with highlight protection. Each photo keeps its own baseline and adjustment. Turning matching off and back on recalculates it; the toggle can be copied to another photo.
- Choose finished output dimensions in Export. Existing custom simulation sizes remain in effect; use **Film → Process Actions** to restore the original simulation size.
- Ruler haptics were refined. JPEG, PNG, and TIFF carry the library rating in `xmp:Rating`. About lists release notes and bundled licences.

### Materials and engine
Experimental includes 29 Fotufilm profiles adapted through Noah Haasis's Negs, under CC BY-SA 4.0. Mobile shares the desktop DIR, Hanatos and Cineon neutral-probe, grain-softening, and neutral print-filtration updates. Existing edits may look different if re-rendered with an affected print pairing. [Data sources](../../license/DATA-SOURCES.md) · [macOS 1.3.0 notes](../../modern_UI/Spektrafilm/releases/1.3.0.md).

### Distribution and validation
- Xcode confirmed upload of build 10 to App Store Connect on November 20, 2026; that does not establish public availability. See the [distribution record](../APP-STORE.md).
- Public iOS arm64 Release build passed. All three components report 1.3.0 (10). The package contains 63 public material profiles; hashes, notices and the extension catalogue were checked for the 29 new films.
- iPhone 17/iOS 26.1 simulator: seven pre-film, crop/simulation-size, and XMP checks and 33 clipboard checks passed. Two build-10 UI regressions covered the hidden simulation-size control, restoration of existing custom sizes, and pre-film exposure.
- macOS regression: 540 passed, none failed, 48 skipped for missing photos or disabled opt-in cases. Real RAW brightness match, haptics and full-resolution export still require device testing.

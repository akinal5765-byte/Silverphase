# Silverphase iOS／iPadOS 1.3.1（构建 11）

**中文 · [English](#english)**

主 App、控制中心入口和锁屏相机扩展采用同一版本和构建号，系统要求为 iOS／iPadOS 18 或更高。安装版本以 App Store 和 TestFlight 显示为准。[分发记录](../APP-STORE.md)

- 「预处理」保留入片影调和受支持 RAW 的相机亮度匹配。「胶片」集中管理测光与手动胶片曝光。应用内相机的 EV 用作随照片保存的胶片曝光补偿。设置剪贴板按对应面板传递。
- 29 款公开 Fotufilm 胶片均有封面与来源标识。Velvia 50 和 400D 等方形封面的角标在裁切后绘制，编辑器和相机选片器都能完整显示。移动端采用和 macOS 相同的 DIR 边缘修正，限制 17 款配置的空间邻接强度，并保留均匀曝光下的响应。

模拟器和未签名的设备构建均通过。已读取设备构建内三个组件的版本号。共享引擎的验证范围包括合成边缘和均匀色块，真机观感和实拍胶片对照仍待验证。[控制审计](../CONTROL-AUDIT.md) · [DIR 测试与限制](../../modern_UI/Spektrafilm/VALIDATION.md#fotufilm-dir-边缘增强复查)

## English

The app, Control Center camera control, and locked camera extension all use version 1.3.1 (build 11). iOS/iPadOS 18 or later is required. App Store and TestFlight determine which version is installable. [Distribution record](../APP-STORE.md).

- **Prep and Film:** Prep retains pre-film tone and camera brightness matching for supported RAW files. Film now owns metering and manual film exposure. EV captured in the in-app camera follows the photo as film exposure compensation. Copy and paste follow the corresponding control groups.
- **Fotufilm:** All 29 public stocks have covers and source badges. Square covers such as Velvia 50 and 400D draw the badge after cropping, so it stays visible in the editor and camera pickers. Mobile shares the desktop DIR adjustment for 17 profiles, limiting spatial adjacency effects without changing the uniform-exposure response.

The simulator and unsigned device build passed; versions of all three device components were inspected. Engine verification used synthetic edges and uniform patches. Real-device appearance and physical film comparisons remain unverified. [Control audit](../CONTROL-AUDIT.md) · [DIR tests and limits](../../modern_UI/Spektrafilm/VALIDATION.md#fotufilm-dir-边缘增强复查).

# Silverphase iOS／iPadOS 1.4.0（构建 12）

**中文** · [English](#english)

主 App、控制中心相机入口和锁屏相机扩展使用相同版本。最低系统要求仍为 iOS／iPadOS 18。各渠道的可安装版本以 [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6829987567) 或 [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/uwY3ZYJp) 显示为准。

## 更新内容

- **图库浏览。** 点开照片先进入独立的浏览页，可滑动切换、缩放、长按对比原片，还能切换胶片、查看信息、评分、标旗或导出；需要时再进入编辑器。图库可按现有文件格式筛选。针对照片和 RAW 较多的图库，减少重复排序和列表刷新工作。
- **相机与选片。** iPhone Duo 腰平取景可用纵向胶片堆叠，滑动时切换近似预览；展开横屏也有专用选片区域。改进不同握持方向下的相机选片器，并让图库和相机弹窗在空间允许时多显示几排。
- **显示。** 在支持 ProMotion 的 iPhone 上，系统可按需使用更高刷新率。

## English

The app, Control Center camera entry, and locked camera extension share version 1.4.0 (build 12). Requires iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. The [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6829987567) and [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/uwY3ZYJp) determine which builds can be installed.

### What’s new

- **Library:** Open photos in a dedicated viewer to swipe, zoom, compare with the original, change film, inspect metadata, rate, flag or export. Open the editor when you need deeper edits. Filter the library by file format and browse large photo and RAW collections with less repeated list work.
- **Camera:** Browse film in a vertical stack with a responsive approximate preview in the iPhone Duo waist-level view, or use the dedicated unfolded landscape chooser. The camera picker better follows device orientation, and both pickers display more rows when space permits.
- **Display:** Supported iPhones can use higher ProMotion refresh rates when appropriate.

- iPhone 17 Pro 与 Duo 曾验证图库入口、菜单和胶片按钮的测试包，iPad 全屏横竖布局和交互在模拟器验证。分屏跨宽度切换、最终分发签名和 App Store Connect 上传属于独立检查，不把模拟器结果写成真机验收。
- Test packages were used on iPhone 17 Pro and Duo for photo navigation, menus, and film controls. Full-screen iPad portrait/landscape layouts and interactions were checked in the simulator. Split view, distribution signing, and upload are separate checks; simulator results do not establish device acceptance.
